FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does this create real public short links?

Yes. Short links are created through the backend and stored in PostgreSQL. The backend also provides the redirect endpoint for your short URLs.

Where are my links stored?

Your links are stored in the PostgreSQL database and associated with your authenticated account.

Are the analytics real?

Yes. Link click counts are tracked by the backend. Each redirect updates the click count and records click history in the database.

Can I create a custom short code?

Yes. You can enter a custom code when creating a link. The code must be between 3 and 10 characters and use supported letters, numbers, underscores or hyphens.

Can I use this on mobile?

Yes. The layout, navigation, controls and animations adapt for smaller screens, with heavy effects reduced on mobile.
